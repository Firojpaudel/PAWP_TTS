# Literature Review

Systematic survey of the 26 most relevant papers, architectures, compression methods, and open-source baselines grounding **PAWP-TTS**.

| # | Work / System | Date | Reference | Core Architecture / Method | Key Findings & Limitations for PAWP |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **PiVo-1.4M** | 2026 | [Empero](https://empero.org/research/pivo/), [HF](https://huggingface.co/empero-ai/PiVo-1.4M) | `1,401,562` total params (`585K` acoustic @ 10 ms + `817K` FARGAN vocoder @ 16 kHz, 2.5 ms subframes) | Strongest full-graph 1.4M English CPU streaming baseline (Apache-2.0 / BSD); 16 kHz only, monolingual English |
| 3 | **NepTTS-Bench & NepaliMOS** | 2026 | [arXiv:2611.03718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03718) | `205` sentences, `77` minimal pairs, `180` native raters (`6,962` ratings), `IndicWav2Vec` MOS predictor | Exposes Hindi phonological bleed missed by SCOREQ; NepaliMOS $\rho=0.90$ dense but $\rho=0.43$ LOSO (proxy only, not human replacement) |
| 4 | **FARGAN** (Valin, Xiph) | 2024 | Xiph / Opus | `~820K` params, `~600` MFLOPS, 20-dim features (18 BFCC + pitch + voicing), 2.5 ms subframes @ 16 kHz | Ultra-low-FLOP streaming neural vocoder; natively 16 kHz (scaling to 24 kHz alters subframe compute) |
| 5 | **Vocos** (Siuzdak) | Jun 2023 (ICLR 2024) | [arXiv:2306.00814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00814) | ConvNeXt1D backbone predicting Fourier magnitude + phase → iSTFT @ 24 kHz | Order-of-magnitude faster than time-domain GAN vocoders; blueprint for our 24 kHz streaming decoder |
| 6 | **iSTFTNet** (Kaneko et al.) | Mar 2022 | [arXiv:2203.02395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02395) | Lightweight Conv1D + iSTFT mel vocoder | Replaces expensive sample-level upsampling with iSTFT for fast CPU inference |
| 7 | **OmniVoice** (k2-fsa) | 2026 | [GitHub](https://github.com/k2-fsa/OmniVoice), [HF](https://huggingface.co/k2-fsa/OmniVoice) | `~0.6B–1.2B` Qwen3 diffusion LM + 24 kHz RVQ audio tokenizer, 600+ languages | Strong zero-shot cloning & multilingual teacher; 400×–800× too large for edge deployment |
| 8 | **Kokoro-82M / `kokoro-ne`** | 2025 | Hugging Face | `82M` StyleTTS 2 derivative; community Nepali+EN code-mix fine-tunes (`misaki`/`espeak-ng`) | Fast CPU teacher/baseline; still >50× larger than PAWP-1.5M |
| 9 | **AI4Bharat Indic Parler-TTS** | 2025 | [HF](https://huggingface.co/ai4bharat/indic-parler-tts) | Prompt-conditioned Transformer TTS supporting Nepali & English | Rich multi-style Indic teacher; autoregressive decoding limits TTFA |
| 10 | **Hima TTS (`hima-tts-nano-hi`)** | 2025–2026 | [HF](https://huggingface.co/Prahaladha/hima-tts-nano-hi) | `~62M` Hindi TTS with ONNX & MLX builds | Useful Indic deployment baseline; **Hindi-only** (never use as a Nepali pronunciation teacher) |
| 11 | **SPADE** (KAIST / 42dot) | Sep 2025 | [arXiv:2509.20802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20802) | WER-based Layer Importance (WLI) + multi-level KD for LLM-TTS | Halves depth with `< 5%` recovery data; coarse whole-layer dropping on huge LLM-TTS, not phoneme-level rank allocation |
| 12 | **MIT CSAIL prune-tts** (Lai et al.) | ICASSP 2022 | [Project](https://people.csail.mit.edu/clai24/prune-tts/) | Unstructured sparsity + TTS-Augmentation + KD | Proves TTS models are over-parameterized; unstructured sparsity rarely yields CPU/ONNX latency gains |
| 13 | **Spotify TTS Distillation** (Henriksson et al.) | Aug 2025 (SSW) | [Spotify Research](https://research.atspotify.com/2025/8/scaling-transformer-based-text-to-speech-with-knowledge-distillation) | Distilling Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) out of Transformer TTS | `> 50%` smaller, `2x` faster without perceptual quality loss |
| 14 | **FWSVD** (Hsu et al.) | ICLR 2022 | [arXiv:2207.00112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00112) | Fisher-weighted low-rank SVD | Beats standard SVD on LMs; uses global scalar Fisher unaware of rare phonetic contrasts |
| 15 | **Basis Sharing** (Wang et al.) | Oct 2024 (ICLR 2025) | [arXiv:2410.03765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03765) | Cross-layer SVD shared basis vectors + unique per-layer coefficients | Closest mathematical prior to PAWP shared-basis pooling; lacks adaptive residuals and phonetic weighting |
| 16 | **ResidualTransformer** (Wang & Li) | Oct 2023 | [arXiv:2310.02489](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02489) | Cross-layer shared full-rank matrix + unique low-rank residual + diagonal scaling | `~3x` ASR encoder compression; uniform residual rank, untested in sub-1.5M bilingual TTS |
| 17 | **AdaLoRA** (Zhang et al.) | Mar 2023 (ICLR 2023) | [arXiv:2303.10512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10512) | SVD-parameterized ($P \Lambda Q$) adaptive rank pruning via sensitivity | Closest adaptive rank allocator; targets PEFT adapters rather than full shared-basis speech synthesis |
| 18 | **RigL** (Evci et al.) | Nov 2019 (ICML 2020) | [arXiv:1911.11134](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11134) | Magnitude pruning + instantaneous-gradient dynamic sparse regrowth | Closest dynamic regrowth baseline; unstructured sparsity, blind to pronunciation errors |
| 19 | **ASVD** (Yuan et al.) | Dec 2023 | [arXiv:2312.05821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.05821) | Activation-aware weight scaling prior to SVD | Mitigates activation outliers during post-training SVD |
| 20 | **SVD-LLM** (Wang et al.) | Mar 2024 | [arXiv:2403.07378](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07378) | Truncation-aware data-whitening SVD | Maps singular truncation directly to compression loss |
| 21 | **Task Arithmetic / TIES-Merging** | Dec 2022 / Jun 2023 | [arXiv:2212.04089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04089), [arXiv:2306.01708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01708) | Weight-space task vectors & sign-conflict resolution | Theoretical grounding for bilingual weight superposition ($W_{\text{shared}} + \Delta W_{\text{lang}}$) |
| 22 | **IMS Toucan / LAML** (Lux & Vu) | ACL 2022 | [GitHub](https://github.com/DigitalPhonetics/IMS-Toucan) | Articulatory phonological feature vectors + meta-learning for low-resource TTS | Universal articulatory features aid cross-lingual transfer; untested in sub-1.5M streaming TTS |
| 23 | **FastSpeech 2 / FastPitch** | Jun 2020 / ICASSP 2021 | [arXiv:2006.04558](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04558) | Non-AR TTS (`~27–50M`) with explicit duration/pitch/energy variance adaptor | Core non-AR variance-adaptor design scaled down `~30x` for PAWP |
| 24 | **LightSpeech / Nix-TTS** | Feb 2021 / Mar 2022 | [arXiv:2102.04040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04040), [arXiv:2203.15643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15643) | NAS depthwise-separable TTS (`1.8M` acoustic) & distilled VITS (`5.23M`) | Early compact TTS milestones; English-only, >3×–5× larger with vocoder included |
| 25 | **VITS / Piper / Matcha-TTS / StyleTTS 2 / F5-TTS** | 2021–2024 | [2106.06103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06103), [2309.03199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03199), [2306.07691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07691), [2410.06885](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06885) | `10M–336M` reference acoustic/flow-matching/VITS families | Upper-bound quality anchors & Piper Nepali reference in NepTTS-Bench |
| 26 | **HiFi-GAN / Multi-Band MelGAN** | May–Oct 2020 | [arXiv:2010.05646](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.05646), [arXiv:2005.05106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.05106) | `13.9M` MRF-GAN & `1.91M` multi-band vocoder (`~0.95` GFLOPS) | Classical time-domain neural vocoder complexity anchors |
