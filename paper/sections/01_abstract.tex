\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{PAWP-TTS}, an ultra-compact, streaming bilingual neural text-to-speech (TTS) system supporting English, Nepali, and English--Nepali code-mixed speech synthesis at 24\,kHz without a large language model at inference time. By offloading deterministic pronunciation rules into an engineered bilingual phonological frontend (105,868-word Nepali lexicon, 76 context-dependent heteronym rules, and a unified 128-token IPA inventory that prevents Hindi phonological bleed), we dedicate the entire neural capacity---including embeddings, variance adaptors, acoustic backbone, and ConvNeXt1D+iSTFT waveform decoder---to compact acoustic realization. Counting every learned inference parameter, our flagship \textbf{PAWP-1.5M} model uses 1,490,390 parameters (5.98\,MB FP32; 5.16\,MB folded ONNX; 1.11\,ms p50 CPU streaming TTFA), achieving 4.02\%/1.50\% English WER/CER, 12.35\%/1.75\% Nepali WER/CER, and 5.04\% Nepali / 4.02\% English Phoneme Error Rate (PER) on 600 untouched test utterances. A decoder-centric \textbf{281K-parameter} tier (1.12\,MB FP32; 1.32\,MB folded ONNX) reaches 5.54\% Nepali / 5.24\% English PER (dense) and 6.28\% / 7.09\% (shared-basis) in single-seed evaluation, with multi-seed verification ongoing. We systematically evaluate mechanistic, zero-training Weight-Space Programming (WSP) edits on compact TTS and report three methodological lessons: (i) apparent phoneme confusions must be audited against label-version drift and native human recordings (where human Kathmandu Nepali exhibits 90.3\% dental realization of orthographic retroflex \textit{na}), (ii) language-residual ROME null-space projection achieves exact 0.000\% cross-lingual collateral and 27:1 pause selectivity with 96 parameters, and (iii) global log-duration under-prediction bias (-7.3\%) must be controlled against simple 1-parameter scalar calibration before claiming mechanistic PER gains. Code, ONNX graphs, and CC~BY~4.0 weights are publicly released.
\end{abstract}
