\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Non-Autoregressive TTS and Fourier Vocoding.}
The acoustic backbone builds on non-autoregressive variance-adapted synthesis~\cite{ren2021fastspeech2} paired with Fourier-domain ConvNeXt1D + iSTFT waveform synthesis~\cite{siuzdak2024vocos}. Unlike conventional pipelines that exclude a multi-million-parameter vocoder from reported parameter counts, PAWP-TTS counts every learned tensor required for inference from phoneme IDs to 24\,kHz PCM waveforms. Compact TTS work has reduced acoustic models through architecture search~\cite{luo2021lightspeech} and module-wise distillation~\cite{chevi2022nixtts}; PAWP-TTS differs by also counting the waveform decoder and targeting a bilingual low-resource language. Articulatory feature conditioning follows prior low-resource multilingual TTS work~\cite{lux2022toucan}. Phoneme durations are commonly obtained by monotonic alignment search~\cite{kim2020glowtts} or jointly learned aligners~\cite{badlani2022onealign}; on the mixed multi-speaker bilingual data used here a jointly learned aligner collapsed, so CTC~\cite{graves2006ctc} forced alignment is used instead (Section~\ref{sec:exp}).

\paragraph{Model Compression and Parameter Sharing.}
Prior compression methods include Fisher-weighted SVD~\cite{hsu2022fwsvd}, adaptive low-rank allocation~\cite{zhang2023adalora}, dynamic sparse training~\cite{evci2020rigl}, and cross-layer basis sharing~\cite{wang2025basissharing}. This work explicitly distinguishes \emph{unique stored parameters} from \emph{materialized compute parameters} when evaluating cross-layer shared bases against equal-unique-parameter dense baselines.

\paragraph{Mechanistic Localization and Weight Editing.}
Rank-one model editing with covariance whitening (ROME)~\cite{meng2022rome} demonstrated targeted, gradient-free edits in language models, and weight-space programming~\cite{wsp2026} frames such edits as reading and rewriting a network's weights like a program, noting that edits can repair but not create capabilities. This work adapts the inspect--localize--edit--verify workflow to compact bilingual speech synthesis, imposing strict evaluation controls: validation-only hyperparameter selection, one-shot evaluation on untouched test data, paired-bootstrap confidence intervals, matched-norm random edits, and same-parameter gradient fine-tuning controls.
